Privacy Policy

Gellert Support (Agri-Stor Management Company, doing business as Gellert) · Effective October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Gellert Support, the technical support line of Gellert (Agri-Stor Management Company), collects and uses information when you call our support line or receive text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number, from caller ID when you call our support line, or the number you give us.
  • What you tell us on the call: your name, your site, the equipment involved and the problem. Support calls are announced as recorded; we keep the recording and a written summary.
  • Text messages we send you at your request, and any replies you send (including STOP and HELP).

How we use it

  • To answer your support question and send you the documents you ask for (wiring diagrams, manuals, setup sheets).
  • To arrange a callback from a Gellert technician when you ask for one.
  • To improve future technical support: written summaries of calls, with names removed, help our technicians answer similar questions.

We send text messages only when you ask for them during a support call. We never send marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We use service providers only to operate the support line and deliver messages (for example, our phone and messaging carrier), and they may use your information only for that purpose.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any Gellert Support text to stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call us at (208) 423-1782.
